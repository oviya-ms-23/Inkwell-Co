Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our shipping, gift wrapping, paper specifications, and returns.

When will my order ship, and how is it packaged? ▼

We pack all orders within 24 to 48 business hours from our village shop. Everything is wrapped in 100% recycled archival kraft paper, sealed with water-activated tape, and dispatched in compostable mailers — completely plastic-free!

Are your notebooks compatible with wet fountain pen inks? ▼

Yes, absolutely! Our dot-grid journals feature ultra-heavyweight 160 GSM bamboo paper specifically engineered to handle broad nibs, shading inks, and even gouache washes without bleeding or ghosting.

How does the free shipping promotion work? ▼

All domestic orders of $35 or more automatically receive Free Standard Shipping at checkout. Plus, you can stack coupon code HAPPYPAGES to enjoy an extra 10% off your entire order!

What is your return & exchange policy? ▼

We want you to adore your purchase. If a book doesn't capture your heart or a journal isn't quite right, we offer hassle-free returns or exchanges within 30 days of delivery.
